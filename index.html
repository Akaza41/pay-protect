FAQ

COMMON
QUESTIONS

Can't find what you're looking for? Talk to our team.

How does QuickGuard lock a phone?+
QuickGuard installs a lightweight MDM agent on the customer's phone at purchase. When an EMI is overdue, the retailer taps "Lock" on the dashboard and the phone is locked within seconds — regardless of where the customer is.
Does it work on iPhones too?+
Yes! QuickGuard fully supports both Android and iOS devices. Setup is simple and fully guided by our support team for both platforms.
Is the customer's personal data safe?+
Absolutely. QuickGuard only restricts phone usage — it never accesses, reads, or deletes personal files, messages, or photos. All data stays secure.
What if the customer turns off internet?+
QuickGuard queues the lock command and executes it the moment the device comes back online. Retailers are also notified when a phone goes offline.
Is QuickGuard available in Pakistan?+
Yes! QuickGuard is available worldwide including Pakistan. Contact us for Pakistan-specific pricing and onboarding support.
How much does it cost?+
QuickGuard uses an activation code model — you purchase codes per device. Flexible plans for individual retailers, distributors, and NBFCs. Request a demo for exact pricing.
